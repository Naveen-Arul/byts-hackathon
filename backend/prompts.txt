Why this works

This prompt follows a production pattern:

Clear role separation: The Judge Agent does not re-evaluate code.
Deterministic behavior: Explicit scoring weights and conflict resolution reduce randomness.
Guardrails: Prevents hallucinations and double-penalties.
Structured output: JSON is ready for FastAPI and LangGraph without extra parsing.
Few-shot guidance: Shows the model how to resolve common evaluation scenarios.
